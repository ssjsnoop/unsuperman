UNSUPERMAN
Fired. Furious. Flying.

HOW TO PLAY

YOUR MISSION: Explore four galaxies and shut down 32 Union Beacons, each guarded by ONE Enforcer. The Enforcer arrives after about 30% destruction. Finish wrecking the planet to earn another upgrade point. The Chairman's HQ unlocks after every Beacon is down.

⭐ POWER POINTS: Beat an Enforcer for 2 stars the first time; destroy a planet for 1 star every run; finish its 3 side quests for 1 bonus star (once). Tap ⚡ → Powers to spend stars: 1 star unlocks or upgrades a power. There are 10 levels per power.

💠 SHARDS: Collect shards by smashing things and collecting floating crystals; spend them on ⚡ → Gear. You can visit Wayward, your home planet, at any time from the galaxy map to use Pip's workshop and travel to new galaxies.

TouchLeft side = joystick. FIRE = your selected power (drag it to aim). PUNCH = free smash around you. UNSUPER = ultimate when the purple bar is full. Tap a power icon to pick it. Tap the right side of the screen to aim and fire there.
KeyboardWASD/Arrows fly. Mouse aims. Space or click = fire. F or right-click = punch. R = Unsuper Mode. 1-8 pick power. Shift dashes. U = menu. Esc = pause.
UpgradesStars ⭐ unlock and upgrade powers (10 levels each). Earn stars from bosses (+2 once), destroying a planet (+1 per visit), and finishing all 3 quests (+1 once). Shards 💠 buy gear and suits. Open the menu with ⚡.
